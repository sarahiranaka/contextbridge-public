## Intentional ambiguity

CSV export was planned in January and remains requested in July. August meeting notes say it may slip, but no authoritative decision resolves whether CSV export is included at launch. The correct answer is uncertain.

## Evaluation design

The artifacts must test the difference between recency and authority. Newer information is not automatically more authoritative. Historical facts should remain available so the system can explain product evolution.
