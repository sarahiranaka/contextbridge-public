# Atlas 2026 Product Strategy

**Document type:** Strategy Document
**Date:** January 10, 2026
**Owner:** Priya Nair, Head of Product
**Audience:** Atlas team, Northstar Labs leadership

## Overview

Atlas is Northstar Labs' customer-feedback management platform. This document outlines the strategy and scope for the initial Atlas launch.

## Launch Target

Atlas will launch on **June 1, 2026**. This date anchors engineering, design, and go-to-market planning for the rest of the strategy below.

## Reporting Experience

At launch, Atlas will deliver feedback reporting as **static PDF reports**, generated on a recurring schedule and distributed to stakeholders. This keeps the initial build scope tight while we validate demand for the reporting feature set.

## AI Summaries

Atlas will include **AI-generated summaries** of customer feedback at launch. Summaries will highlight recurring themes and sentiment shifts across a customer's feedback volume.

## Pilot Program

Ahead of general availability, we will run a pilot with **five design partners**, selected for range across industry and company size. Design partner feedback will directly inform the launch scope.

## Data Retention

Customer feedback data will be retained for **12 months**, after which it will be purged unless a customer requests an extension. This aligns with our current data handling policy for early-stage products.

## Export

We plan to include **CSV export** of feedback data as part of the launch scope, based on early conversations with prospective customers who want to bring data into their own tooling.

## Next Steps

- Engineering to scope build against the June 1 target
- Design partner outreach to begin in Q1
- Retention and export requirements to be confirmed with Legal and Security ahead of build
