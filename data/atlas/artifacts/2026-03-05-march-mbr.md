# Atlas Monthly Business Review — March 2026

**Document type:** Monthly Business Review (MBR)
**Date:** March 5, 2026
**Owner:** Priya Nair, Head of Product
**Audience:** Northstar Labs leadership

## Summary

Atlas development is progressing against the plan outlined in the January strategy, with one emerging risk to the June 1 launch target.

## Progress

- Core feedback ingestion and tagging pipeline is functional in staging.
- Design partner outreach is underway; three of five target partners have confirmed participation.
- Static PDF report generation is in active development.

## Risk: Engineering Dependency

The AI summarization feature depends on a shared inference infrastructure upgrade owned by the Platform team. That upgrade has slipped by approximately six weeks, which puts the June 1 launch date at risk if AI summaries remain in scope for launch.

Engineering is evaluating two paths: (1) accelerate the Platform team's timeline through additional staffing, or (2) narrow launch scope. Neither path has been decided.

## Launch Date

**No change to the launch date has been approved.** June 1 remains the target while we evaluate mitigation options. We expect to bring a recommendation to leadership by early April.

## Open Questions

- Can the Platform dependency be de-scoped from the critical path without materially changing the AI summary feature?
- What is the cost/impact of a design partner cohort expansion later in the year, if we want broader validation before general availability?
