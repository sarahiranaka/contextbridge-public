# Atlas Launch Timing & Data Retention — Decision

**Document type:** Decision Memo
**Date:** April 2, 2026
**Decision owner:** Northstar Labs Product Leadership (approved by Priya Nair, Head of Product, and David Okafor, VP Engineering)
**Status:** Approved, effective immediately

## Context

As flagged in the March MBR, the Platform team's inference infrastructure upgrade has slipped, putting the AI summarization feature and the June 1 launch date at risk. Leadership reviewed mitigation options and made the following decisions.

## Decision 1: Launch Date

The Atlas launch date is moved from June 1, 2026 to **September 15, 2026**. This gives the Platform team sufficient runway to complete the infrastructure upgrade without cutting AI summaries from launch scope, and gives the design partner program additional time to generate feedback before general availability.

## Decision 2: Data Retention

Based on requirements surfaced during enterprise sales conversations, feedback data retention is increased from 12 months to **24 months**. Security and Legal have reviewed and approved this change.

## Not in Scope for This Decision

This decision does not change the reporting experience, AI summary scope, design partner cohort size, or export plans defined in the January strategy. Those remain as previously scoped unless separately revisited.

## Next Steps

- Engineering to replan against the September 15 date
- Data retention change to be reflected in the updated data handling policy
- Design partner program to continue as planned
