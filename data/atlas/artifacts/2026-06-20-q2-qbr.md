# Atlas Q2 2026 Business Review

**Document type:** Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
**Date:** June 20, 2026
**Owner:** Priya Nair, Head of Product
**Audience:** Northstar Labs leadership

## Summary

Atlas remains on track for the **September 15, 2026** launch. This review covers Q2 progress across engineering, design partners, and go-to-market readiness.

## Engineering

The Platform team's inference infrastructure upgrade is complete, resolving the dependency flagged earlier this year. AI summarization is now in active development against the September 15 date.

## Design Partner Program

All five design partners are actively using Atlas in staging. Early usage patterns show strong engagement with feedback tagging; reporting features are seeing lighter usage so far, which we plan to investigate further next quarter.

## Go-to-Market

Sales and marketing planning is underway for a September launch. Enterprise prospects have continued to ask about data retention and export capabilities during evaluation conversations.

## Looking Ahead to Q3

- Expand qualitative research with design partners to validate the reporting experience ahead of launch
- Finalize AI summary quality benchmarks
- Continue go-to-market planning against the September 15 date
