# Atlas Launch Scope — Decision

**Document type:** Decision Memo
**Date:** August 3, 2026
**Decision owner:** Northstar Labs Product Leadership (approved by Priya Nair, Head of Product, and David Okafor, VP Engineering)
**Status:** Approved, effective immediately

## Context

The July customer research findings identified significant concerns with the planned reporting experience and AI summary quality ahead of the September 15 launch. Leadership reviewed these findings along with updated engineering estimates and made the following decisions.

## Decision 1: Launch Date

The Atlas launch date is moved from September 15, 2026 to **October 15, 2026**. The additional time is needed to rebuild the reporting experience described below and is a direct response to issues surfaced during the pilot.

## Decision 2: Reporting Experience

The planned static PDF reporting experience is replaced with **interactive dashboards** for launch. Customers will be able to explore and filter their feedback data directly rather than receive a fixed report.

## Decision 3: AI Summaries

AI-generated summaries are **deferred to a post-launch release**. Given the quality concerns raised during the pilot, we do not believe summaries will meet our reliability bar in time for October 15, and we would rather ship without them than ship a feature customers don't trust.

## Not in Scope for This Decision

This decision does not revisit data retention or export scope. Those remain governed by prior decisions unless separately reviewed.

## Next Steps

- Engineering to replan against the October 15 date and the interactive dashboard requirement
- Product to define the post-launch roadmap for AI summaries
- Design partner communication to go out this week
