**Document type:** Product Update

## Where We Stand

Atlas remains on track for launch on **October 15, 2026**. Over the past several weeks the team has been heads-down on the dashboard rebuild and finishing pilot feedback loops.

## Pilot Program

Our pilot cohort now includes **20 customers**, up from the original five design partners we started with earlier this year. Their usage and feedback over the past two months have directly shaped where the team has focused ahead of launch.

## What's Next

- Customer-facing launch communications planning begins this month
- Support and onboarding materials being finalized with the pilot cohort's help

## Thank You

Thanks to everyone across product, engineering, design, and the pilot customers themselves for the work that's gone into getting Atlas ready. More updates as we approach launch.
