## Current State

### Launch date
October 15.

The original June 1 target became infeasible because of an engineering dependency. The launch was first moved to September 15 and later moved to October 15 after issues were discovered during the customer pilot.

### Reporting experience
Interactive dashboards.

Atlas originally planned to launch with static PDF reports. Customer research showed that users needed to explore and filter feedback rather than consume a fixed report, so the team changed the launch experience to interactive dashboards.

### AI summaries
Deferred until after launch.

AI-generated feedback summaries were part of the original launch scope. During the customer pilot, the team found that summary quality was not reliable enough for launch, so the capability was deferred.

### Customer pilot
20 customers.

Atlas initially planned to work with 5 design partners. The research program was later expanded into a 20-customer pilot.

### Data retention
24 months.

The original plan specified 12 months of retention. A later approved decision increased retention to 24 months.
